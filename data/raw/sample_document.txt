Python is a popular programming language used in software development, data analysis, and artificial intelligence.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation, also known as RAG, combines information retrieval with language generation. A RAG system searches a collection of documents to find relevant information before generating an answer.

Document ingestion is the process of loading and preparing source documents for later retrieval. Documents are often cleaned and divided into smaller passages called chunks.

A retrieval system ranks document passages according to their relevance to a user's question. Better retrieval can help a language model produce more accurate and better-grounded answers.
