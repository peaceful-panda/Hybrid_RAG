Text embeddings are numerical representations of text. An embedding model converts a sentence, paragraph, or document into a vector containing many numerical dimensions. Texts with similar meanings tend to have vectors that are close together in the embedding space.

Semantic search uses embeddings to find information based on meaning rather than only exact keywords. For example, a question about loading documents may retrieve a passage discussing document ingestion even if the word loading is not present.

The all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model is a commonly used sentence-transformer model for generating compact text embeddings. It produces vectors with 384 dimensions. During retrieval, the question is converted into an embedding and compared with stored document embeddings using a similarity measure such as cosine similarity.
